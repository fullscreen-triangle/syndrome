Dual-threshold criterion.
A peptide is a neoantigen when both conditions hold simultaneously:

1. Δρ_self = ρ(WT, self) − ρ(mut, self) > Δ*
The mutant is spectrally displaced from the self-peptide ensemble — the immune system can distinguish it.

2. ρ(mut, MHC) > ρ*
The mutant binds the MHC groove with sufficient affinity for surface presentation.

PARTIAL means only one criterion is met. NOT_NEOANTIGEN means neither is met.
